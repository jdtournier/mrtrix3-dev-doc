1 Overview {#mainpage}
2 ========
3 
4 MRtrix was developed with simplicity, performance, flexibility and consistency
5 in mind, which has led to a number of fundamental design decisions. The main
6 concepts are explained in the following pages:
7 
8 - The build process is based on a Python script rather than Makefiles, and all
9  dependencies are resolved at build-time. This is explained in @ref build_page.
10 
11 - The configure script allows you to create different co-existing
12  configurations, for example to easily switch from a release build to a debug
13  build. This is explained in @ref configure_page.
14 
15 - You are encouraged to set up your own, separate module on top of the MRtrix
16  codebase. This allows you to write your own code, stored on your own
17  (potentially private) repository, without affecting the MRtrix core repository.
18  This is explained in the section @ref module_howto.
19 
20 - The basic steps for writing applications based on MRtrix are explained in the
21  section @ref command_howto.
22 
23 - The concepts behind accessing and processing %Image data are outlined in @ref
24  image_access.
25 
26 - There are a number of convenience classes to simplify development of
27  multi-threaded applications, in particular the Image::ThreadedLoop and
28  Thread::Queue classes. These are outlined in the section @ref multithreading.
